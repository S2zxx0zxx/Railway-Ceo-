Privacy notice — The CEO: Company Simulator

Release draft, 25 November 2026. Publisher contact and public policy URL must be confirmed before Play distribution.

Local game data

The game stores the company and CEO names you enter, simulated business records, progress, settings, recovery backups and archived companies on your device. Use fictional names if you prefer. The game is a fictional simulation; do not enter real financial information.

Collection and sharing

The current game design does not require an account, advertising, analytics, payment processing, a remote game server or transmission of game saves to the developer. Web hosting may process ordinary connection information when serving the web app or updates; the eventual hosting provider's practices must be disclosed in the final web policy. The app uses local storage and a file picker. If you choose to export or share a file, your selected destination or third-party app receives that file under its own terms. Exported files may contain entered names and game history; they are not encrypted.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until replaced, removed by the browser or device, or you clear the site's storage or the app's data. Clearing storage deletes local backups and archives too. Copies you exported remain wherever you saved them; delete them separately. Android cache files may be removed by the operating system. The developer does not maintain a remote account or game save deletion service in this version.

Changes and contact

Policy changes must reflect actual release behavior. Before public distribution, the publisher must provide a working privacy contact and a public, non-editable policy URL.
