- One distressed manufacturing company, one SKU at launch. Working SKU: 100 g plain packaged biscuit; retained unless the owner explicitly changes it. Grow capacity and distribution before adding other products. Multiple factories, acquisitions and industries are expansion scope; no scope creep.
- Management-first simulation: investigate, restore, procure, produce, release, sell, collect, reinvest, delegate. Real economic constraints; not a click-for-money game. Preserve all 25 corporate systems, 34 experience concepts and 15 panels from V2.0.
- Premium static isometric 2.5D CEO office, original brand and composition, landscape-first; no navigable world, live 3D, character movement, pathfinding, rigging, animated conveyor/truck systems, multiplayer or mandatory AI/backend. Static portraits, machine illustrations, state-based office art and small CSS transitions only.
- Two deliverables from one React/TypeScript codebase: installable offline PWA and bundled Android app through Capacitor, QA APK and signed release AAB. No mandatory server, cloud database or paid APIs. Free static hosting subject to limits; publishing, optional advertising and future cloud services separate.
- No copied proprietary assets, screenshots, game name, brand, or exact third-party icon art. Use the user's screenshots as internal design references only; never package them as game art. Public resource visibility is not permission. No fake controls, balances, charts, data, claims of completeness, silent feature removals, or unverified adoption.

## 4. Business system implementation
### Opening scenario (proposed balancing fixture, not final calibration)
Company takes over a distressed biscuit manufacturer through fictional restructuring. Opening operating cash ₹300,000, loans ₹1,400,000, payroll arrears ₹180,000, 24 employees, eight equipment records including utilities/quality equipment, reputation 12/100, line stopped. A balanced seed chart of accounts must explicitly include inherited assets, AP, liabilities and equity (possibly negative). Cash must have a funding source; do not assume positive equity. Generate a seed opening journal and audit checklist. Numerical assumptions, recipe, machine speeds, costs, demand, tax, timing and difficulty must live in versioned validated data, not scattered literals. Fictional rules are not real corporate/legal advice.

### Authoritative mechanics
- Company: identity and immutable company ID; takeover accepted once; audit gates, owner capital, board approvals and staged rescue; five stages based on solvency, safety, positive cash flow and reach. Bankrupt → recovered → regional → national → global/export. Single site until expansion scope approved.
- Accounting: integer paise using bigint; serialize canonical decimal strings; explicit rounding policy and remainder allocation; all journal entries balance, append-only, unique source event and command IDs. Chart of accounts: cash, AR, raw/WIP/finished inventory, equipment/accumulated depreciation; AP, payroll/tax payable, debt/interest payable; capital/retained earnings; revenue/returns, COGS, wages, OPEX, interest, depreciation, taxes, losses. Opening journal reconciles. Correct with reversal, not edits. Reports derived from ledger; no direct balance setters. Cash/credit limit policy prevents unauthorized spending. Profit and cash separate; liabilities not revenue.
- Posting examples: receipt Dr raw inventory / Cr AP or cash; payment Dr AP / Cr cash; material issue Dr WIP / Cr raw; allocated production labor/overhead Dr WIP / Cr relevant clearing or liability/cash; finish Dr finished / Cr WIP, abnormal rejects Dr loss / Cr WIP; delivery Dr AR / Cr sales and Dr COGS / Cr finished; collection Dr cash / Cr AR; loan Dr cash / Cr debt; accrual Dr interest / Cr payable; principal repayment Dr debt / Cr cash, interest separately; payroll Dr wage expense or WIP / Cr payable; settlement Dr payable / Cr cash. Depreciation Dr expense or WIP / Cr accumulated depreciation. Tax and returns separate. No double counting payroll or overhead. Use recognized revenue at confirmed delivery as the game policy; invoice/collection are separate states.
- Procurement: suppliers have price, reliability, quality, MOQ, credit terms, capacity and lead time. PO created → approved → in transit → received → inspected/accepted or rejected/cancelled. Reserve cash/credit where needed; receipt posts exactly once. Reorder point = forecast daily use × lead time + safety stock. EOQ optional recommendation sqrt(2DS/H) only if inputs positive; clamp by cash, shelf life and capacity. No automatic infinite orders.
- Inventory: quantities stored integer grams/pieces/pack counts; explicit unit conversions; track lot ID, arrival, expiry, cost, location, quality, reservations. FEFO physical allocation (earliest valid expiry first) and moving weighted average accounting by item; reconcile rounding residuals and actual/usable/reserved/WIP quantities. No negative or duplicate reservations; expired or held inventory unavailable. Record shrinkage, rejection, disposal with ledger effects. Rework subject to explicit safe policy; unsafe lots disposed/held.
- Production: one BOM, versioned recipe and yield; raw ingredients (flour, sugar, oil, water etc.) + packaging. Plan → reserve → run staged mix/form/bake/cool/pack → quality hold → release/reject → stock. Each stage constrained by available inputs, labor, machine capacity, utilities and time. Bottleneck sets max throughput; OEE = availability × performance × quality, avoid applying quality twice in output. Mass balance: inputs = output + recorded evaporation/loss/waste in consistent units; packaging separately. Processed quantities and costs conserved. Cancellation returns only unconsumed reservations; consumed costs remain accounted. Production events are discrete scheduled state changes, not visual physics.
- Machines: condition, utilization, rated speed, energy cost, maintenance schedule, technician, parts and repair/upgrade cost. Seeded failure probability tied to wear; repair reduces downtime but consumes cash/time; replacement posts asset changes. Safety and utilities affect eligibility. Engineering/research upgrades alter validated multipliers. No operation with unavailable machine.
- HR: employee skill, morale, attendance, fatigue, wage, role and shift assignment. Hiring/payroll/training/firing with costs and time; deterministic availability and productivity; no person simulation on map. Salary arrears reduce morale; safety and overtime constraints. Managers execute same commands as player within policy and approval budgets. Payroll accrues by defined calendar, settles separately.
- Market: seeded competitor agents with bounded cash/capacity/price, stock, brand and market policy. Demand model with price elasticity, availability, quality, brand and seasonality; softmax/logit share allocation (normalized, stable exponent, no demand created by rounding). Competition reacts at market intervals, not each animation frame. Clamp values, test monotonic isolated price response; no guaranteed ads ROI. Material prices and supplier shocks bounded. Market total divided without duplication; no AI service.
- Sales: offer → accept/reserve → dispatch → deliver → invoice → collect; cancellations release unused reservation, returns/recalls reverse revenue and handle stock/refunds under policy. Show price, margin, delivery deadline, credit period and cash timing. Delivery cost/lead time/availability simulated, no moving truck. AR overdue affects cash/credit. Complaints and on-time fulfillment update reputation.
- Banking: deterministic loan offers depend on credit, collateral, cashflow and debt; annual rate converted explicitly to period rate; amortization and interest schedule, due dates, rounding; missed obligations produce default/negotiation. Overdraft explicit liability and limit. Asset sale and investor injection have costs/ownership implications; deposit optional. All operations post once.
- Research: unlock tree, prerequisites, bounded benefits, cost and completion timer; improve existing product, shelf life (validated fictional model), yield, quality, energy and automation; no paid advisor. Campaigns budget and duration, marketing return bounded and uncertain.
- Crisis: event record cause/severity/deadline/options/eligibility/cost/outcome; derived triggers (machine wear, arrears, shortage, failed quality, debt), seeded external shocks (supplier delay, inflation, competition, disasters). Dedupe by causal ID/cooldown. Response effects atomically post state and ledger. Bankruptcy staged liquidity alert → distress → default → restructuring window → recovery or liquidation. Beginner checkpoint assistance; Realistic/Hardcore stricter rules. No pay-to-rescue.
- Compliance/safety/environment: fictional licenses/inspection schedule, hygiene, quality recalls, workplace incidents, energy/waste costs, audit and approval controls; simplified but meaningful. Not a regulated accounting/industrial controller product.
- Automation: bounded manager budgets/authority, reorder thresholds, shift coverage, min margin, release requirements; manager cannot override safety, stock, accounting or user approval. Explain decision log. In absence of manager, only committed orders continue when allowed. Achievements based on actual milestones. No compulsory daily login or reward loop.

### Clock, event ordering and offline policy (proposed defaults)
Use a single deterministic engine and seeded PRNG state; no Date.now()/Math.random() within domain. Wall clock read only in platform adapter. Time represented integer simulated minutes; initial selectable speeds pause/1x/5x/20x/60x subject to balancing. Save last wall timestamp, sim timestamp, speed, seed and revision. At same timestamp process due obligations, receipts, maintenance/HR updates, batch events, quality, shipping, invoices/collections, market updates, policy decisions and milestones in a documented stable priority + sequence order. Events generated at current timestamp must have a bounded processing rule; reject cycles. Foreground worker advances elapsed time, not frame count. Offline catch-up on resume uses saved policies and the same event logic, never promises background process. Proposed cap 24 real hours initially; show excess discarded and time anomaly notices; negative elapsed → zero, clock jump bounded, paused save stays paused; local clock tampering cannot be fully prevented. Yield in chunks (target <50ms UI stall), resume interrupted checkpoint safely, persist exactly once; report production, sales, cash, expenses, downtime, alerts and skipped duration. Same elapsed interval partitioned into chunks must yield equivalent state and journal under the same inputs. No duplicates on double resume or multi-tab use.

UI emits typed command with saveId, expectedRevision, unique commandId and payload → authority validates using latest state → pure transition returns proposed domain events, journal and state changes → persistence transaction verifies revision and idempotency and commits them together → updated projection published to UI. Worker can calculate proposals but cannot independently mutate persistence. UI never sets cash, inventory, reputation or progress directly. Single-writer lock or transactional compare-and-swap; stale tabs reload safely. Use strict schemas for all inputs; no remote executable configuration. Persist structured game state, journal, event queue, unique command/event IDs, revision, clock/RNG, config version and metadata. Storage is local and user-editable; no competitive or real-money security claim. Save envelope includes schema version, content version, game build, checksum, snapshot, journal, time and RNG. Export with explicit user action; checksum detects accidental corruption, not malicious tampering. Import validates size/schema/references/invariants before atomic replacement; never overwrite a good save with invalid import. Keep rotating backups and migration rollback/recovery; do not clear storage on update or uninstall claims. Browser storage may be evicted; warn and encourage export. ## 10. Acceptance gates and operational evidence
G01 Scope: all D and E IDs map to implementation/approved deferred status; no silent omissions; every M panel has tested actions. G02 resource safety: pinned commit, actual file paths, test evidence, dependency/license review and asset provenance; excluded code absent. G03 UI: owner-approved original visual baseline; layout and touch/focus tests; screenshots of all 15 panels on target sizes. G04 accounting: debits=credits, balance sheet equation, no double counts, postings exactly once, AR/AP/stock reconcile; opening and end-to-end fixture. G05 manufacturing: no negative quantities, material/packaging conservation, no unapproved/expired sale, reservations atomic, downtime/utility/labor constraints. G06 market: bounded normalized shares, demand responsiveness, no unlimited cash, reproducible competitors and events. G07 recovery: at least one viable initial rescue path per supported difficulty; bankruptcy and recovery outcomes consistent. G08 offline: seeded replay parity, chunk equivalence, pause/cap/time anomaly, crash/reopen/import/update no duplication or save loss. G09 automation: same validation as user, budget/authority, stop conditions, decision log. G10 platform: real PWA install/offline update, native QA APK physical-device test and save transfer, no network for core. G11 release: signed AAB and verified metadata, Play target and policy checks, third-party notices, no secrets, no high-priority defects. G12 evidence: task ledger records exact revision, checks and artifacts; no release without owner approval.

Test scenarios: opening audit → creditor deferral → wage settlement → repair → PO on credit → receipt → valid production → QC hold/release → order reservation → dispatch/delivery → invoice → cash collection → repay → reports. Alternate insufficient funds, expired material, failed QC, canceled order, refund, simultaneous duplicate command, machine breakdown, overdue salary, loan default, recover or liquidate. Offline across the same events must match foreground simulation. Tests include rounding at high balances, zero demand, no staff, capacity zero, credit sales without cash, migration from older saves, interrupted catch-up, stale tabs and low-storage error. No claim of exhaustive verification until executed.
